The problem

Corporate responsibility fails when it’s a press release instead of an operating decision.

Most companies want to do good. Fewer know how to build a program that holds up when a journalist, an investor, or their own workforce starts asking questions.

We work with organizations that are ready to stop performing social impact and start operating it — with governance, measurement, and reporting that stands on its own.

Founder

Tyler Butler

Tyler has spent more than a decade building social impact programs for companies including Microsoft, GoDaddy, Lyft, Nissan, Bridgestone, and Shell — and writing about the practice as a contributor to Forbes, Fortune, and Fast Company.

She founded Collaboration for Good in 2023 to do the work directly: helping organizations build responsibility programs that are operationally real, publicly defensible, and genuinely useful to the communities they touch.
